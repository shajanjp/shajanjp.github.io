Morse code practice, powered by real English words.

morsed is a speed-focused Morse code trainer that uses real words — not random characters — so you build muscle memory for the patterns that actually matter. Type dit (`.`) and dah (`-`) to spell words against a ticking clock, then review your CPM, accuracy, and streak in the results.

## Features

**Typing with real words.** Words are drawn from a curated bank of common English words across lengths. Each session generates a fresh sentence-like sequence that mixes short, medium, and long words for natural variety.

**Timer-based sessions.** Choose 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 3 minutes. Your stats tick in real time, and the session ends when the clock hits zero.

**Dit & dah input.** Press `.` for dit and `Space` or `-` for dah. On touch devices, the on-screen dit/dah buttons appear. You can also click/tap the input area — short press for dit, long press or right-click for dah.

**Audio feedback.** Each dit and dah plays its corresponding tone. Correct inputs advance the character with a subtle pop animation. Wrong inputs trigger a low buzz, flash the error state, and reset the buffer after a brief delay.

**Real-time stats.** The top bar shows countdown timer, characters per minute, accuracy percentage, and current streak — all updating live during the session.

**Results screen.** At the end of every session, you see:
- Characters per minute
- Overall accuracy
- Total time
- Characters typed and words completed
- Best streak
- A CPM chart plotting your speed over time
- The full session sentence with per-word color coding (correct words in amber, errors in red)

**Character history bar.** The bottom strip shows your most recently typed characters alongside their Morse code patterns, making it easy to review what you just entered.

**Mute toggle.** Turn audio on or off at any time.

**Dark, minimal design.** Inspired by Monkeytype, the interface uses mono-spaced typography (Roboto Mono), a dark canvas, restrained amber accents, and generous negative space around the central typing area.

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| `.` key | Dit |
| `Space` or `-` key | Dah |
| Left-click (input area) | Short = dit, long (>120ms) = dah |
| Right-click (input area) | Dah |
| Mute button | Toggle sound |
| 30s / 1m / 3m buttons | Change session duration |
| Logo | Start new session |
| Enter / Escape (results) | Start new session |
